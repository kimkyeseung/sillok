# Thumbnail Image Prompts: Foreign Figures Who Shaped Korean History

Target size: 1200x630 (OG image). No text in the image. No depictions of specific real people.
Style direction: Cute, comic-style, playful but historically themed.

---

## Option 1: Chibi World Map

**Style:** Kawaii/chibi cartoon illustration with thick outlines, soft shading, and rounded proportions. Think Doraemon meets history textbook.

**Composition:** A cute, simplified Korean peninsula sits at the center like a little island, wearing a traditional gat (hat). Around it, tiny chibi-style characters in period costumes approach from all directions — a round Mongol warrior on a stubby horse from the north, a miniature French admiral on a comically small warship from the west, a tiny suited general with oversized binoculars from the south, a chibi Chinese emperor riding a cloud from the east. Each character has exaggerated dot eyes and blushing cheeks. Small comic effects: speed lines, sweat drops, question marks, and sparkles scattered around.

**Color palette:** Pastel sky blue background. Warm peach and soft green for the peninsula. Each character group in a distinct pastel tone — lavender for Chinese figures, mint for Japanese, soft coral for Western. White fluffy clouds.

**Mood:** Adorable, lighthearted, inviting. History made approachable and fun.

---

## Option 2: Comic Panel Mashup

**Style:** Retro manhwa (Korean comic) style with halftone dots, bold panel borders, and dynamic action lines. Slightly exaggerated proportions — big heads, small bodies, expressive faces.

**Composition:** A single wide image divided into 5-6 comic panels of different sizes, overlapping slightly. Each panel shows a different era as a tiny comic scene: a fortress with a flag and tiny soldiers bouncing off it (Goguryeo), a round Mongol horseman chasing a running Goryeo figure, a comically oversized Western ship arriving at a tiny Korean port, cute missionaries carrying an enormous cross up a hill, two cartoon generals arm-wrestling across the 38th parallel. The panels overlap and tilt at playful angles. Small sound effects in Korean comic style (but as visual symbols, not actual text).

**Color palette:** Cream/aged paper background for the panels. Bold primary colors — red, blue, yellow — with black ink outlines. Halftone dot patterns in the shadows. Pop of hot pink and electric blue for action effects.

**Mood:** Energetic, fun, pop-art. Like flipping through a history comic book at high speed.

---

## Option 3: Cute Convoy Parade

**Style:** Flat vector illustration with a storybook quality. Clean lines, simple shapes, warm and friendly. Similar to Google Doodle or Kurzgesagt style.

**Composition:** A horizontal parade of cute, simplified historical figures marching in a line from left to right toward a smiling Korean palace gate. The figures are arranged chronologically: first a round Chinese emperor in flowing robes, then a puffy Mongol on a chubby horse, a tiny samurai with an oversized sword, a small Western sailor with a telescope, a miniature missionary with a big hat, and finally modern soldiers with round helmets. Each figure is about the same cute size regardless of historical importance. They walk on a gently rolling green hill under a cheerful sky with puffy clouds. Small details: cherry blossoms, a tiny Korean flag on the palace, birds.

**Color palette:** Soft warm tones — buttercream sky, sage green hills, terracotta and dusty rose for the figures. Each era subtly shifts in hue from warm amber (ancient) to cool teal (modern). Gold accents on the palace.

**Mood:** Warm, welcoming, storybook-like. History as a friendly, colorful parade.
